ABOUT

A simple QR tool built to stay fast and useful.

This QR Code Generator was created for people who want to make a professional QR code quickly without creating an account or fighting through unnecessary steps. The focus is practical: generate a code, customize it, verify that it scans, and export it in a format that works for digital or print use.

The tool supports common QR types such as websites, text, WiFi, contact details, WhatsApp and social links, plus custom colors, center logos, different shapes and high-resolution downloads.

What we care about

  • Useful first: the generator should remain easy to use even as more advanced features are added.
  • Privacy: static QR creation should not require an account.
  • Print quality: QR codes should be exportable in formats suitable for labels, cards, signs, packaging and marketing materials.
  • Reliability: customization should not come at the cost of scanability.
