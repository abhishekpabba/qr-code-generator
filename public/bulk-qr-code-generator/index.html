Bulk QR code generator

Bulk generation is useful when you need dozens or hundreds of QR codes for products, badges, invitations, labels, inventory or campaigns. Instead of entering each destination manually, prepare a CSV and generate the set in one workflow.

Prepare your CSV

Keep one QR destination per row. Use clear column names such as name and url so downloaded files can be matched back to the correct record.

Common use cases

  • Product packaging and SKU labels
  • Event badges and attendee links
  • Table numbers and restaurant locations
  • Direct-mail campaigns
  • Asset and inventory labels

Before printing a large batch

Test a sample of the generated codes, especially if you are applying logos or non-standard colors. For commercial print, SVG or PDF is generally preferable when available.
