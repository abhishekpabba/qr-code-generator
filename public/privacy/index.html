Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

QR Code Generator is designed to work with minimal personal data. Static QR codes are generated in your browser and do not require an account.

Information we process

When you use static QR generation, the content you enter is processed in your browser to create the QR code. We do not require you to create an account.

If you choose a dynamic/trackable QR code, the destination URL and basic scan information may be stored so the redirect, editing, and analytics features can work. Scan analytics may include time of scan, general device or operating-system information, referrer information when available, and a privacy-protected network identifier.

Local browser storage

The site may store recent QR codes and design preferences in your browser using local storage. You can clear this information through your browser settings.

Uploads

If you upload a logo or center image for a static QR code, it is used to generate the QR design. We do not use uploaded images for advertising or unrelated purposes.

Analytics and advertising

We use Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate website traffic, including visits, page views, popular pages, referrers, general location, browser, operating system and device type. This helps us improve the service and understand when the site has enough usage to support advertising. We do not use this analytics data to identify individual visitors.

If advertising services are added later, this policy will be updated to describe the relevant cookies or similar technologies.

Data retention

Static QR content is not intentionally stored by our application server. Dynamic QR information is retained while the dynamic QR remains active or as needed to operate the service.

Your choices

You can use static QR generation without signing up. You can also clear locally stored QR history through your browser. If account-based features are added later, this policy will be updated.

Changes

We may update this policy as the service evolves. The latest version will always be posted on this page.
