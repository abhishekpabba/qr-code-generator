Why use SVG?

SVG is a vector format, so your QR code can be enlarged for posters, packaging, signs and professional print without becoming blurry.

SVG vs PNG

Use PNG for most web and social uses. Choose SVG when you need unlimited scaling or want to edit the QR artwork in a vector design application.

Print tip

Keep strong contrast and enough quiet space around the code, and always test the final printed QR code before producing a large batch.
