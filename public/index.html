QR code FAQ

Are these QR codes free?

Yes. Static QR codes created here can be downloaded and used without creating an account.

Do static QR codes expire?

No. A static QR code keeps working as long as the information encoded inside it remains valid.

Which file format should I use?

Use PNG or JPG for websites and social media. Use SVG or PDF when you need sharp print output at larger sizes.

Can I add a logo or social icon?

Yes. Upload your own image or choose a built-in center icon such as Instagram, Facebook, Website or TikTok.

Will a customized QR code still scan?

The generator checks the finished QR image and warns when contrast or customization may reduce scan reliability.

What is a dynamic QR code?

A dynamic QR code points to a redirect URL so its destination can be edited later and scans can be measured. Dynamic QR codes are planned as an advanced feature.
