Create a WiFi QR Code

How it works

  1. Open the QR generator and choose WiFi.
  2. Enter the SSID, password and security details.
  3. Customize the design, test the code and download it.

Do WiFi QR codes expire?

Static WiFi QR codes do not expire. They will keep working until the network credentials change.

Where can I use one?

WiFi QR codes are useful for homes, restaurants, hotels, offices, events, rentals and guest networks.
